# Getting help

- **Questions about a specific repository**: open an issue there, or use its Discussions tab if enabled.
- **Community conversation**: join the [Active Inference Institute Discord](https://discord.activeinference.institute).
- **Learning Active Inference**: start at [Start](https://activeinferenceinstitute.github.io/Start/), the [courses](https://github.com/ActiveInferenceInstitute/courses) repository, and the [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ActiveInference/).
- **Programs, partnerships, and everything else**: see [activeinference.institute/get-involved](https://activeinference.institute/get-involved/) or email [blanket@activeinference.institute](mailto:blanket@activeinference.institute).

Most projects are maintained by volunteers; please be patient.
