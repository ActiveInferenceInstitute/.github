as *known limitations* in [`standards/README.md`](standards/README.md) are listed under "Owner
decisions" because tightening them is a policy choice, not a bug.

## Known defects / unverified behaviour

- [ ] **Required-context naming is unverified.** Whether a caller job's `name:` or a composite
      `<caller> / <callee>` string is reported is untracked by
      `prove-reusable-workflow-naming-and-canary`. Until that experiment reports, adopting the
      reusable workflows on a gated repository cannot be confirmed to reproduce its required
      context. Affects `python-ci.yml`, `node-ci.yml`, `julia-ci.yml`, `html-ci.yml` and
      `gnn-conformance.yml`.
- [ ] **Nine required contexts are not reproducible** by `python-ci.yml` (for example
      `Journal_Utilities` `"test"`, `GEN24` `"tests"`, `GEO_INFER` `"probes (...) (ubuntu-latest, 3.15)"`).
      Those repositories keep their own jobs until an adoption template for each shape exists.
      Enumerated in the header comment of [`python-ci.yml`](.github/workflows/python-ci.yml).
- [ ] `scripts/audit_required_contexts.py` and `tests/test_audit_required_contexts.py` exist
      **untracked** as owner work-in-progress. They are not part of the committed tooling and are
      not covered by the suite. Decide whether to track, finish or remove them.

## Environment prerequisites

- [ ] The blocking gate needs a token: `--github` reads the three `CORE-INHERIT-*`
      `github-api` criteria, and an offline `--check` exits `3` by design. Use
      `GH_TOKEN="$(gh auth token --user ActInfInstitute)"`.
- [ ] The fleet report and the ledger need the owner's **scope table** (a `repo<TAB>rel` TSV over
      the vault). It is configuration, not repository content, and is deliberately not committed
      here. `build_public_ledger.py` and `check_ledger_boundary.py` cannot run without it.
- [ ] `uv sync --frozen` requires network on a cold cache; after that the tests, lint and
      typecheck are offline.

## Owner decisions

- [ ] Catalogue version `1.1.0` is current; repository manifests may lag it (the field is
      informational). Decide whether to chase every adopter's `standard_version`.
- [ ] `CORE-CI-ON` measures trigger presence, not that `push` targets the default branch. Making
      it check the branch is a new non-blocking criterion under the ratchet, not a tightening.
- [ ] A reusable-workflow call job cannot declare `timeout-minutes` (`actionlint` rejects it), so
      `CORE-CI-TIMEOUT` reports an unfixable failure for that shape. The bound belongs to the
      called workflow.
- [ ] The `org` tier does not measure that each workflow is `on: workflow_call` only;
      `ORG-REUSABLE-WORKFLOWS` only proves lint-clean. A dedicated criterion would need review.
- [ ] Publish the generated `standards/public-ledger.json` only through
      `scripts/build_public_ledger.py`; never hand-edit it.
